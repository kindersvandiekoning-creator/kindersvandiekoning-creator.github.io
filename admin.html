Welcome 👋

Use the menu on the left to manage the website.

  • 📅 Events — add birthdays, prize-givings, anything coming up.
  • ✉ Newsletters — write a newsletter, save it, and send it to everyone on the list.
  • 👥 Staff — add or edit who's on the Our Staff page.
  • 🏗 New school — post progress updates (with a photo) as the new building goes up.
  • 🖼 Site images — change the big photos on the home page and the other pages.
  • 📬 Subscribers — see who is on the mailing list. Export to a spreadsheet if needed.
  • 💬 Queries — read messages sent from the Contact page.

Everything you add here shows up on the public website within a few seconds.
