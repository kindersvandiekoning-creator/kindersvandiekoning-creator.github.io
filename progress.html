Vision & mission

A permanent home for the children.

For nearly three decades we have rented halls, shared spaces, and made-do with prefab classrooms and a Wendy-house office. The new building will give the children classrooms that are truly theirs, a garden where they can run, and a future they can count on.

Our vision is simple: a safe, Christian Early Childhood Development Centre where children from the Casablanca and Sercor Park 2 communities can learn, eat, play, and be loved — every morning, every term, every year. We work in partnership with the Dutch Reformed Church Gordon's Bay, and every milestone, every load of bricks, every answered prayer will go on this page.

The story so far

A quick history

Kinders vir die Koning began in the late 1990s as a small outreach to pre-schoolers in Strand. Over the years the centre moved between borrowed and rented spaces, eventually settling into the Community Hall of Casablanca — which we rent from the City of Cape Town — together with four prefab classrooms and a Wendy-house that serves as office and storeroom.

Today we care for 70 children between the ages of 3 and 6, five mornings a week, guided by six educators and supported by a cook, a caretaker, helpers, and volunteers. We follow the CAPS programme, serve two meals a day, and take the children on excursions to nature reserves and other places of interest.

The next chapter is a permanent school building — so that the work of the last 20+ years can continue for the next generation of Strand's children.
